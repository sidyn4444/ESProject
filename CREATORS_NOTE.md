# Creator's Note

## Climate "why"

I noticed more heat and humidity this summer, even in Fall, so i'm a little worried about the future.

## Climate superpower

Using coding and talking to people to make a useful program about extreme heat.

## Audience

College students, friends, and family in North Carolina, especially people who spend time outdoors.

## Creator's note

I made NC Heat Helper because this summer I noticed how hot and humid it was getting in North Carolina and it made me worry about the future. I wanted to use my coding skills to make something useful that shows people how humidity can make the heat feel worse. The app is meant for people my age, friends, and family because we all spend time outside whether it's walking or playing sports. I also added information from North Carolina's climate report and some tips so people can understand why this matters and what they can do about it.
